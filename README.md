# Mars Navigator: Interplanetary Survival Guide (Martian Map)

NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 project by **Pravya Sharma** (team lead), **Advika Bisaria** and **Kshama**.

- `index.html`: the main map (layers, mission objectives, route planner, temperature zone clock)
- `globe.html`: slide-sized 3D globe with a fixed pin, directions, distances and a suit meter
- `guide.html`: the field guide, safety layer, solar power plan, team credits and all sources
- `data.js`: place names (USGS/IAU Gazetteer) and NASA rover waypoints (built-in copy)
- `crew.js`: crew chat and field camera (demo mode, data stays in the browser)
- `img/`: NASA images (credited on the guide page) and team diagrams

Map imagery: NASA/JPL Mars Trek. Libraries: Leaflet (BSD-2) and three.js (MIT), loaded from unpkg.
Prepared by the team with help from an SI assistant for coding and fact-checking; checked by the team.
NASA does not endorse this project.
